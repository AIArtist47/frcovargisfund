## Direction contract

THESIS: The fund as the parish's own family album: the people Fr. Vargis raised, laid out photograph by photograph on light leaves and captioned by hand. Refuses the charity template (full-bleed smiling-children hero, impact counters, testimonial carousel) and the cream editorial page it replaces.

OWN-WORLD: Light album leaves on the client-pinned nine-step gray palette (#F8F9FA ground through #212529 ink, nothing else); photographs unframed and straight with soft 16px corners, and 16px on every card (client-pinned, 2026-10-06); captions in pencil (Kalam); linen-textured doors, #212529 for supporters and #495057 for scholars; Montserrat Bold for every header (client-pinned), Mukta for text; a glassine interleaf for overlays. No cards, no eyebrows, no stat tiles.

STORY: The visitor opens the album, sees whose fund this is (Achen and the family he raised), picks a door (give or study) on the first leaf, then turns leaves: Achen's life, the family register, the two doors in full, the dinner, what a degree does, gatherings, and a signed colophon.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Light leaf. Left six columns: Montserrat wordmark in the header, a left-aligned display title "Educate one, lift a family.", one line of lead, then two linen doors side by side (#212529 give / dinner / sponsor; #495057 who qualifies / application status), stacking below 480px. Right side: the icon painting ending above the hero edge with a short falloff at its left and bottom; the young Vargis photograph (16px corners, no frame) set over its lower edge, and a pencil caption on plain paper below. Nothing centered.

SIGNATURE: Mounting. Prints settle onto the leaf as they enter (lift, then press down to their hand-set angle as the shadow tightens), and each pencil caption writes on after its print lands. One orchestrated moment; static under reduced motion.
