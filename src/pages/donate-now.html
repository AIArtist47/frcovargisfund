Is my gift tax-deductible? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. The Fr. C.O. Vargis Scholarship Fund is a registered 501(c)(3), so contributions are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

Where does my gift go? {{icon:plus}}

To scholarships for under-privileged students in India pursuing professional degrees, and to building a sustainable fund that can support students in perpetuity.

Can my company sponsor? {{icon:plus}}

Yes. A Scholar Sponsor ($4,000) or table host ($2,400) reserves a table of eight at the anniversary dinner. Write to scholarship@frcovargisfund.org.

Who can I ask about a gift? {{icon:plus}}

Email finance@frcovargisfund.org and someone from the board will get back to you.
