What we look for, drawn from the Fund's mission.

  1. 01Students

    First-generation college students in India whose families live below the poverty line.

  2. 02Studies

    Professional degrees, with a focus on STEM. Past scholars have earned degrees in nursing, pharmacy and computer engineering.

  3. 03Where

    Our initial focus is Kerala, with hopes of expanding to students in other states.

  4. 04Support

    Full-ride scholarships that remove the financial barrier between a capable student and a finished degree.

  5. 05Fairness

    Every applicant is considered without regard to color, creed, gender or religion.
