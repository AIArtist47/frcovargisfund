A priest who believed in the classroom.

The fund carries the name and the conviction of Fr. Dr. C.O. Vargis, known to his community as Vargis Achen.

  • more than 50 years a priest of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church
  • over 20 years vicar of St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church, Houston
  • founding vicar of the first Orthodox parish in Houston
  • retired in 2014 as Vicar Emeritus of St. Stephen’s

The namesake of the fund, Fr. Dr. C.O. Vargis, is the inspiration behind its purpose and vision. He served as vicar of St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church for over twenty years and has been a priest of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church for more than fifty.

To his community, “Vargis Achen” is more than a spiritual leader. His humor, his positive attitude, and his gift for making every person feel loved, and as if they were his personal favorite, made him part of the family for hundreds. He is a pioneer of the Indian Orthodox Church in the United States and was the founding vicar of the first Orthodox parish in Houston.

Throughout his life he has advocated for the education of children in the United States and India, and personally financed numerous students. He did not only preach higher education; he lived it, earning seven degrees over his lifetime, the last a doctorate at the age of 65.

His love for Christ, his church and his flock, and his belief that an education can not only edify one person but uplift an entire family out of poverty, have been the driving force behind his work. When he retired as Vicar Emeritus, his family and community chose to carry that work forward for generations through this fund, building a lasting source of hope for those who need it most.
