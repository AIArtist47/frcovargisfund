The people keeping the promise.

Most of our board grew up under Father Vargis’s care. Today they are physicians, educators, an engineer, an attorney and nonprofit leaders.

  1. Liju Mathew

    President

    Read his story {{icon:plus}}

    Liju is the Major Gift Officer at the Houston Food Bank and has extensive experience in the nonprofit sector, with prior roles at One&All, BakerRipley and, earlier, the Houston Food Bank. He received his BA in Public Relations from Stephen F. Austin University in 2004. Liju grew up in Houston at St. Stephen’s parish, where Fr. Vargis was, and continues to be, a significant influence in his life.

  2. Jaison Thomas

    Treasurer

    Read his story {{icon:plus}}

    Jaison serves on the Construction Committee of St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church, has served as parish Trustee, and has served on past and current Leadership Conference committees. He has worked in petroleum engineering since 2008 and is a Production Engineer at Birch Resources. He received his BS in Petroleum Engineering from Texas A&M University in 2008 and his MBA from the University of Florida in 2014. Fr. C.O. Vargis is Jaison’s godfather and has been a deeply influential part of his life.

  3. Dr. Toby Thomas

    Scholarship Chair

    Read his story {{icon:plus}}

    Toby serves on the Managing Committee of St. Thomas Orthodox Cathedral. He is an Emergency Medicine physician in the Houston area and an Assistant Professor in the Department of Emergency Medicine at the University of Texas Health Science Center at Houston. He received his BS in Biomedical Science from Texas A&M University in 1999, his MD from the University of Texas Medical Branch at Galveston in 2003, and trained in Emergency Medicine at the University of Florida, Jacksonville, in 2006. Toby is the godson of Rev. Fr. C.O. Vargis, who has been part of his life since the day he was born.

  4. Dr. Rachel (Deepa) Ittiara

    Board member

    Read her story {{icon:plus}}

    Deepa practices outpatient general internal medicine in Chicago and is an Assistant Professor of Medicine at the University of Illinois Chicago (UIC). She is the VA outpatient site director for UIC’s third-year Internal Medicine clerkship and mentors and volunteers at the UIC student-run free clinic. She earned her BS and MD from the University of Oklahoma and completed her Internal Medicine residency at Loyola University Medical Center in Chicago. As Father Vargis’s niece, she learned the importance of community from his example and credits much of her academic success to his encouragement.

  5. Shirly Thomas

    Board member

    Read her story {{icon:plus}}

    Shirly has spent her career in the nonprofit sector and works at The Holdsworth Center, which serves public education leaders in Texas. In philanthropy she has supported outcomes-based giving by building relationships and encouraging collaborative strategy. Passionate about equitable access to education, she recently served on the board of the Texas Campaign to Prevent Teen Pregnancy. Shirly holds a Master of Public Health from Boston University and grew up in Houston at St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church, Fr. C.O. Vargis’s parish.

  6. Thomas Daniel

    Board member

    Read his story {{icon:plus}}

    Thomas J. Daniel is the Managing Partner at Kwok Daniel Ltd., LLP. He earned his law degree from the University of Texas in 1996, attending on scholarship, and his bachelor’s degree in economics from the University of Houston, magna cum laude. Fr. Vargis has been a significant influence in Thomas’s life, and Thomas helped start the Fund after Fr. Vargis retired.

  7. Jessy Kuruvilla

    Board member

    Read her story {{icon:plus}}

    Jessy has been an educator for over 25 years and is certified EC–12. She earned a Bachelor’s in Education (1997) and a Master’s in Education Administration (2000) from Chestnut Hill College in Pennsylvania. She began her career teaching high school science, English and history in inner-city Philadelphia and has since taught every grade level on the East Coast, in the Midwest and in Texas. She continues to work in education, prison ministry and other nonprofits in the Dallas–Fort Worth area. Jessy grew up in Houston with Vargis Achen as her vicar from 1976; his belief that every child deserves the chance to reach their full potential became her focus.

  8. Ann Nina Mathen

    Board member

    Read her story {{icon:plus}}

    Nina is an educator with more than 20 years of experience. As owner and operator of a Kumon Center, she has taught reading and mathematics to learners from age three through adulthood. She also serves on the board of the Pasadena Education Foundation, supporting the 67 campuses in the district. Nina holds a Master’s in Mathematics and Computer Applications and a Bachelor’s in Mathematics.
