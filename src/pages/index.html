The inspiration

Vargis Achen, teacher by example.

For more than fifty years Fr. C.O. Vargis has served as a priest of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church, including over twenty years as vicar of St. Stephen's Orthodox Church in Houston. He was the founding vicar of the city's first Orthodox parish.

He preached higher education and lived it, earning seven degrees and quietly paying for many students' studies himself. When he retired in 2014, his family and community founded this fund to carry that work forward.

What we do

Built to last.

A fund designed so that each year's giving becomes a permanent source of hope.

  1. 01Mission

    To give financial support to under-privileged students in India pursuing a professional degree, and to build a lasting fund for those living beneath the poverty line.

  2. 02Vision

    A self-sustaining fund, endowed in perpetuity, so that our effort stays focused on the students who need it.

  3. 03Where

    We begin in Kerala, India, with hopes of reaching under-privileged youth in other states in the years ahead.

  4. 04Promise

    The Fund does not discriminate or show bias based on color, creed, gender or religion.
