Vargis Achen, teacher by example.

For more than fifty years Fr. C.O. Vargis has served as a priest of the Malankara Orthodox Syrian Church, including over twenty years as vicar of St. Stephen’s Orthodox Church in Houston, where he was the founding vicar of the city’s first Orthodox parish.

He preached higher education and lived it, and quietly paid for many students’ studies himself. His belief has never moved: an education can not only edify one person, but uplift an entire family out of poverty.

When he retired in 2014, his family and community founded this fund to carry that work forward for generations.

The family he raised.

Our board includes Achen’s godsons, his niece and parishioners who grew up in his care. Today they are physicians, educators, an engineer, an attorney and nonprofit leaders.
